## longmemeval_m: measured

- 470 answerable questions: knowledge-update 72, multi-session 121, single-session-assistant 56, single-session-preference 30, single-session-user 64, temporal-reasoning 127.
- Per history (every 10th of them, 47 measured): median 475 sessions, 16,058 EventMemory segments at 500 characters, 1,019,511 tokens.

## longmemeval_m

- **Documents:** One user's chat history with an assistant per question: ~500 sessions (M) of user and assistant turns, sessions dated; filler sessions from ShareGPT and UltraChat, evidence sessions LLM-simulated and human-screened (about 70% human-edited)
- **Queries:** Questions about the user's past conversations (single-session user/assistant/preference, multi-session, knowledge update, temporal reasoning), LLM-drafted and rewritten by human experts (~5% of drafts kept)
- **Gold:** Every turn marked has_answer (1.9 per question; 94% are user turns, median 66 tokens; assistant evidence turns median 246), all needed; the evidence sessions as a second, session-level view
- **Scope:** history, because a chatbot searches the memory of the user it is talking to: each question's own history
- **Variants:** `question` the question as asked
- **What it adds:** The chat-memory case EventMemory serves: long per-user histories, dated sessions, questions needing one or several sessions
- **Caveats:** Questions and evidence sessions involve LLM generation (human-rewritten and screened). The 30 abstention questions have no answer in the history and are excluded. Gold is whole turns: long assistant turns make 'complete' strict, which is why 'touched' and 'fraction' are reported too.
- **Source:** https://huggingface.co/datasets/xiaowu0162/longmemeval-cleaned
