# The core's measurements at MemMachine's runtime pool — longmemeval_m · seg500 · 470 questions

**How to read the numbers** (definitions: METHODOLOGY sections 2 and 8.10)

- **Limit k:** the results returned, MemMachine's `limit` (default 20), the same on both sides. The first stage returns its first k seeds. **The runtime pool** reranks the first 4 × k (MemMachine's overfetch) and keeps k. **A flat pool**, for comparison, reranks the first 200 and keeps k.
- The reader reads the k results in order until its token budget (Qwen3 tokenizer, the rendered context). Coverage: the share of questions with all the evidence read in full (every) within the budget.
- The reranker's cost: its measured pair tokens over the pool it scores, at DeepInfra's list price.
- Δ: paired by question, with its 95% bootstrap interval. *Best k* is chosen on the same questions, so it leans optimistic.
- The 2026-11-12 report's flat 200 (L 200) differs in one more way: it read all 200 reranked seeds until the budget, with no limit k. It is shown where it is the comparison.
- Recall at top-k (the k results, no budget): `longmemeval_m__history__question__all__runtime_topk.md`.

## Summary

Coverage (every: all evidence read in full) at 500, 1,000, 2,000, 4,000 reader tokens. Δ: reranked minus the first stage at the same limit k, paired by question, with its 95% interval.

With a token budget, a larger k only adds candidates: the reader stops at the budget either way. So the best k is the largest, 50, whose runtime pool is 200: the 2026-11-12 report's setting, with the same numbers.
